## Full description
Bob is not leaving his house.

The zombies come down the road in waves. Bob shoots by himself, so you never have to aim. Your job is the shopping: a better gun, a taller fence, a moat, a crocodile for the moat, and then a hippo, because why stop.

Every upgrade changes something you can see. The house grows from one floor to six. Neighbours move into the windows and help in their own way: Tom with his rifle, Grandma Maria with her rolling pin, Sofia the chemistry teacher with flasks nobody should drink, DJ Max with speakers the size of doors.

- 100 waves, with a named boss every ten
- More than 120 upgrades in six categories
- Three skills to tap at the right moment: sprinklers, an exorcism and an airstrike, and later an orbital laser
- Zombies on bikes, in cars and helicopters, and several kinds of dinosaur
- Three difficulty levels
- Plays offline
- Free to play, and it can be finished without paying

Optional purchases: faster game speed, automatic skills, no ads, a Sandbox mode where everything is free, and coin crates.
