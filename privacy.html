Bob's House Defense: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Bob's House Defense" uses, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by Alexandros Psarris (HungryBob), Greece ("we"). Contact: hungrybob3000@gmail.com.

In short

  • You do not need an account to play, and we do not ask for your name, email address or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your own device.
  • The version in the App Store and Google Play shows ads and offers purchases. Those are handled by the companies named below.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves the following on your device so that you can continue where you stopped: the wave you reached, your coins and upgrades, the difficulty you chose, your settings (sound, music, vibration), your choice about ads, and which extras you have unlocked. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it.

You can create a backup code in Settings. The code contains your game progress only. It is shown to you and is not sent to us.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when a page is loaded, such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement for details. The web version contains no advertising, no analytics and no trackers, and it loads nothing from other companies.

The App Store and Google Play versions

Saving to your account. So that your progress comes back after you reinstall the game or change phone, a copy of your game progress is kept with your Apple account (iCloud) or your Google account. It is stored by Apple or Google under their own privacy terms. We do not receive it.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made. We never receive your card or payment details.

Advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, the ad provider may collect your device's advertising identifier, your IP address, general information about your device, and how you interact with ads. Before the first ad, the game asks whether ads may be personalised. If you choose "Not personalised", the ad provider is told not to use your advertising identifier to tailor ads to you. You can change your choice at any time in Settings. On iPhone, personalised ads also require your permission in Apple's own tracking prompt.

Buying "No ads" removes all advertising from the game.

Legal basis (European Union)

Storing your progress and settings is necessary to provide the game you asked for. Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Children

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you delete the game, clear the browser's data, or use "Start over". Information held by Apple, Google or the ad provider is kept according to their policies.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority). Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, most requests are best addressed to Apple, Google or the ad provider, but you can always write to us at hungrybob3000@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
